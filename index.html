The Origin

About Me

A senior BI business analyst who believes every dataset has a story worth telling.

I'm a Senior Business Intelligence Business Analyst and Team Lead with 3+ years of delivery experience across reporting, dimensional data modeling, and stakeholder-led solution design. I translate business needs into testable BI requirements — developing source-to-target mappings, validating transformation logic, and building high-performance Excel and Power BI datasets with DAX and Power Query (M).

My path started in IT support and asset management, moved through data and business analysis, and led to leading a team of analysts today — facilitating stakeholder workshops, defining KPIs and business rules, and guiding dashboards from requirement to release.

I hold a B.Com in Information Technology Management and I'm currently completing a Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management, both through MANCOSA, alongside certifications spanning Azure, Power Platform, advanced Excel and data analysis.

Explore the experience and projects below to see that work in practice, and get in touch if you'd like to collaborate.
