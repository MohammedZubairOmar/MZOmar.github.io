About Me

A business analyst who believes every dataset has a story worth telling.

I'm a dedicated Business Analyst with a strong track record of using data to drive business efficiency and insight. My work spans multiple industries, with a focus on data analysis, process automation, and business process improvement.

With a degree in Information Technology and several industry certifications, I've built a solid foundation in analyzing business processes, optimizing workflows, and shaping data-driven strategies. Excel, VBA, and the Power Platform are my everyday tools for delivering automated reporting systems, custom solutions, and clear data visualizations.

I'm passionate about finding innovative solutions to complex business problems and continuously improving operational efficiency — whether that means streamlining a process, automating a routine task, or surfacing an insight that was hiding in plain sight.
